Privacy Policy

Applies to ProScan extension version 2.1 and this website · Last updated September 24, 2026

The short version

The ProScan extension saves what it scans in your own browser. ProScan has no server that receives your scans. The one exception is the AI chat: if you add your own Google Gemini API key and ask a question, your question, the recent chat, what you scanned and a list of the products it found go straight from your browser to Google. Cloud sync is turned off in version 2.1. We never ask for your Amazon login, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

ProScan is a Chrome extension for Amazon product research, plus this website and a web dashboard. It is built and run by one developer, Enes Yilmaz. If anything here is unclear, email enesyilmaz5157@gmail.com.

What the extension reads from Amazon

The extension only runs on amazon.com. It reads search result pages and seller storefront pages when you start a scan. For each product it saves:

  • The title, ASIN, price, star rating, review count, Prime badge and product link.
  • The time it was scanned.
  • The page address you scanned, which includes your search keyword or the seller's ID.

It does not read your Amazon account details, your orders, your addresses, your payment details, or anything in Seller Central. It does not collect personal information about buyers or sellers.

Pages the extension loads for you

Every scan starts when you click the button. Once a scan is running, the extension does load Amazon pages on your behalf:

  • Pagination. It moves your tab to the next page of results, one page after another, until it reaches the last page, the page limit, or you press Stop.
  • Price spread. If you run a price spread check, it requests the "other sellers" offer page on amazon.com for each product in your scan and saves the prices those sellers are asking.

These requests go only to amazon.com. They are made from your browser, so they carry your normal Amazon cookies, exactly as if you had opened those pages yourself. Amazon sees them as your traffic. ProScan never reads, stores or sends those cookies. Nothing runs in the background when you are not scanning.

What is stored on your device

The extension keeps its data in Chrome's local extension storage, on your computer. That includes:

  • The products from your most recent scan, and the price spread results if you ran that check.
  • The last price, rating and review count seen for each product, kept to compare against your next scan.
  • Details about the current scan, such as the pages it has covered.
  • Your settings, and your Gemini API key if you added one.

None of this is sent to ProScan. Starting a new scan replaces the previous scan's results. Exports (Excel, CSV and JSON) are saved as files in your downloads folder, and ProScan does not keep a copy anywhere else. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

AI chat and Google Gemini

The chat panel only works if you paste in your own Google Gemini API key. There is no built-in key. Without a key, the chat sends nothing.

With a key, each time you ask a question the extension sends this to Google's Gemini API:

  • Your question.
  • A list of products from your current scan: title, ASIN, price, rating, review count and Prime badge.
  • What you scanned: the search keyword or seller storefront ID, and how many products the scan found.
  • The last few questions and answers from this chat session, so Gemini can follow the conversation.
  • Your API key, so Google knows which account the request belongs to.

The request goes directly from your browser to Google. It does not pass through ProScan, and we never see your questions, the answers or your key. Google is the processor here, and its handling of the data is set by the Gemini API terms for your key. If your key is on Google's free tier, those terms let Google use what you send to improve its products. Your key stays in local extension storage until you remove it or uninstall ProScan.

Accounts, the dashboard and cloud sync

In extension version 2.1, cloud sync is off. The extension does not sign in, does not upload scans, and does not talk to ProScan's database.

This website has a dashboard you can sign up for, with an email and password or with Google. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. If you make an account, we store:

  • Your email address, a display name taken from your Google profile or your email, and when the account was created.
  • Anything you type into the dashboard yourself, such as notes and lead stages on products.

This lives in Cloud Firestore, a Google database, under your account. Other users cannot read it. We use it only to run the dashboard for you.

A later version of the extension will offer sync to the dashboard. It will be opt-in: nothing will upload unless you sign in to the extension with an account and turn sync on. We will update this page before that version ships, and it will say exactly what gets uploaded.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until a new scan replaces it, you remove it, or you uninstall the extension.
  • Dashboard accounts: until you ask us to delete the account. There is no automatic expiry.
  • Gemini requests: kept by Google according to the terms of your API key. ProScan keeps nothing.

Deleting your data

To remove extension data, uninstall ProScan. To delete a dashboard account and everything stored under it, email enesyilmaz5157@gmail.com from the address on the account, and we will delete it. There is no self-serve delete button yet.

Who else handles data

  • Google Gemini API, for AI chat, only when you add your own key.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and Hosting), for this website and the dashboard.
  • Amazon, which receives the page requests your browser makes during a scan.

We do not sell or rent data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for ads or credit decisions. This website runs no analytics or ad trackers.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

ProScan's use of the data it handles complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use data only to provide the features you see, and no human reads it unless you ask us to, for example to help with a support request.

Earlier versions

Versions 2.0 and older had a built-in Gemini key owned by ProScan. On those versions, chat questions and the list of scanned products went to Google under that key. The key has since been revoked, so those versions can no longer send chat requests. Version 2.1 removes the built-in key.

Children

ProScan is a tool for sellers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

When ProScan starts handling data in a new way, this page will change first and the date at the top will move. If a change affects people with a dashboard account, we will also email them.
