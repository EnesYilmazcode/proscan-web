Scan Whole Result Sets

Start a scan on a search or a seller's storefront and ProScan works through the result pages for you, collecting each product's title, ASIN, price, rating, review count and Prime badge.

Opportunity Scores

Each product gets a score built from its rating, review count and price, and ProScan flags well-rated products priced far below the rest of your scan.
